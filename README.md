## 🧭 Executive Summary

All three dashboards tell one consistent story: **2024 was a soft year, and 2025 was a recovery — but a narrower and more fragile one than the headline numbers suggest.**

Sales and profit dipped in 2024 and rebounded in 2025, but the 2025 rebound came from **fewer, higher-value orders** and a **shrinking, more concentrated customer base** — not broader demand. Beneath this cycle, two problems have worsened every year regardless of the ups and downs: **customer retention** and **loss-making orders in Paper**.

### 🔑 Key Findings

| Insight | Detail |
|---|---|
| 🖥️ **Technology is the core engine** | Drives the most sales, the sharpest swings, and sits in the top profit-margin tier all three years. Its 2024 dip explains most of that year's company-wide decline. |
| 🏠 **Home Office overtook Consumer** | The top segment since 2024, confirmed independently in both the Overview and Customer dashboards — a real shift in NexaRetail's buyer base. |
| 📉 **Retention is in freefall** | Repeat customer rate fell for three straight years: **60.78% → 59.00% → 58.70%** — the only metric in the entire review that moved in one direction. |
| 📄 **Paper's losses are compounding** | Loss-making orders rose **60%** in three years (15 → 18 → 24), tied to a broader pattern where discounting thin-margin items (paper, pens, sofas, tables) tips them into losses. |
| ⚖️ **2025's recovery is narrow** | Sales, profit, and margin hit 3-year highs — but customer count and order count both fell. Growth came from **value per transaction**, not more customers or transactions. |

## 📂 Report Structure

The full review is organized into three linked dashboards, each following a **What Happened → Why It Happened → What To Do** framework:

### 1️⃣ Overview: Sales, Profit & Margin
The company-wide V-shape — how Technology's swings and Furniture's counter-movement shaped 2023–2025, and how Home Office permanently overtook Consumer as the leading segment.

### 2️⃣ Product & Profitability Analysis
Where losses hide. Paper's loss-making orders climbed every year (+60%), while Tables improved sharply — proof the pattern is fixable. Profit collapses sharply the moment any discount is applied, especially on thin-margin subcategories.

### 3️⃣ Customer & Order Analysis
Retention is quietly eroding beneath a stable headline customer count. The top 5 customers by sales are entirely different people each year, and 2025's higher Average Order Value masks a shrinking core customer base.

---

## ✅ Prioritized Action List

1. **Fix retention before chasing more growth** — the only metric that worsened three years straight.
2. **Resolve Paper's losses and cap discounting** on thin-margin subcategories (paper, sofas, tables, pens).
3. **Protect and diversify beyond Technology** — build Furniture as a second growth engine.
4. **Design specifically around the Home Office buyer** — now the leading, and stickiest, segment.
5. **Manage customer concentration risk** — a shrinking pool of high-spending customers now carries outsized weight.
